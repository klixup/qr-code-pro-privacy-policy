Privacy Policy — QR & Barcode Scanner Fast

Last updated: November 2, 2026

QR & Barcode Scanner Fast is a private, on-device Android app. Your scan history, contacts, Wi-Fi details, and created codes belong only to you. No account is needed to use the app.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan QR codes and barcodes in the viewfinder. Camera frames are processed on your device with on-device barcode scanning. No video or photo is recorded, stored, or sent to a server.

Photos

When you choose Scan from Image, the system Photo Picker gives temporary access to the image you pick. The image is read on your device only, to extract a barcode. It is never sent off your device.

Data on your device

Scan history, created QR codes, and favorites are stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing is synced to the cloud. QR images you save go to Pictures/QRCodePro on your device. Bulk QR ZIP exports you choose go to Downloads/QRCodePro on your device.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or remote analytics. It collects no data and shares no data with third parties. Scanned links are never opened on their own. A link opens only when you tap it.

Your control

You can delete single records, or clear your full scan history, favorites, and created codes at any time from Settings.
